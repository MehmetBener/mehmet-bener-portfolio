Services

From napkin sketch to fab-ready files.

Focused on 1- and 2-layer boards for makers, educators and small hardware teams, where a careful layout keeps cost low and the first batch working.

Schematic capture

Your block diagram or prototype, turned into a clear, annotated schematic with parts you can actually buy.

Layout and routing

    Process

    Clear steps, no surprises.

    You approve the schematic and the layout before anything is final. A typical small board takes one to two weeks.

    1. 01

      Brief

      What the board does, size, connectors, budget and quantity.

    2. 02

      Schematic

      Parts chosen for stock and price. You review and sign off.

    3. 03

      Layout

      Placement, then routing. A 3D render goes to you for approval.

    4. 04

      Verification

      DRC against your fab's rules, footprint and BOM cross-checks.

    5. 05

      Hand-off

      Fab files, the KiCad project, and help placing your order.

    Deliverables

    Upload, order, done.

    Every project ends with a complete manufacturing package that uploads straight to PCBWay, JLCPCB or any other fab and assembly service.
